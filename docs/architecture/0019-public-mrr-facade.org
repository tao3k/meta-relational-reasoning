#+title: RFC 0019 - Public MRR Facade
#+status: accepted

* Surface

=meta-relational-reasoning= exposes one =MrrEngine= builder and flat MRR
semantic types.  It does not reexport backend crates as modules.  Consumers do
not name Ascent, Gerbil, GQL AST, parser tables, or native ABI details.

#+begin_src mermaid
flowchart LR
  B[Validated ReasoningBundle] --> E[MrrEngine]
  E --> Q[query]
  E --> D[derive + materialize]
  E --> Y[why + why-not + impact]
  E --> S[check safety]
  A[admit facts] --> E2[New immutable MrrEngine]
#+end_src

Engine admission is functional: adding facts validates and returns a new
engine.  It never mutates or publishes live state.  Deduction is configured by
=DeductionPlan= and =DeductionLimits=, which hide the execution backend while
preserving explicit budgets.

#+begin_src typst
#set page(width: 150mm, height: auto, margin: 12mm)
$ E = "MrrEngine"("ReasoningBundle") $
$ E' = E."admit"("facts") $
$ E != E' quad "and" quad "live-state"(E) = "none" $
#+end_src

Lineage and transition owners retain validation authority.  The facade only
composes their immutable outputs after exact identity and generation binding.
