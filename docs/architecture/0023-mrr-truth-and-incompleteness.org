#+title: MRR Truth and Incompleteness
#+startup: overview

* Status

Normative for public MRR conclusion states.

* Truth states

MRR exposes six disjoint states:

- TRUE: a complete authority-bearing receipt establishes the conclusion.
- FALSE: complete bounded analysis establishes that the conclusion does not hold.
- UNKNOWN: evidence coverage is not complete enough to decide.
- INCOMPLETE: evaluation stopped because of a cycle or explicit budget.
- STALE: the selected intent or revision no longer matches current content.
- CONFLICT: admitted authorities provide incompatible claims.

These states are not aliases. UNKNOWN, INCOMPLETE, STALE, and CONFLICT never
project to FALSE or TRUE.

* Owner projections

WHY-NOT owns missing-premise and evidence-coverage analysis. Missing premises
and no applicable rule project to FALSE only when its bounded analysis is
complete. Incomplete evidence coverage projects to UNKNOWN. Cycle, depth,
expansion, and alternative limits project to INCOMPLETE.

The transition checker owns safety status. Safe projects to TRUE, unsafe to
FALSE, and a state or transition budget stop to INCOMPLETE. A budget stop is
never published as safe.

Intent binding owns freshness. Current projects to TRUE and stale to STALE.
Explicit authority conflicts project to CONFLICT. The public facade performs
only these typed projections and does not become a second proof authority.
