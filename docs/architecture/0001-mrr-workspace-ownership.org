* Status

Accepted foundation.

* Decision

This repository is a language-neutral Meta-Relational Reasoning workspace.
GQL and Cypher are language adapters; neither owns MRR identity, relation,
logic, transition, lineage, or bundle semantics.

The dependency direction is executable documentation in the Mermaid Babel
block below. In particular, =mrr-lineage= and =mrr-transition= are siblings
over =mrr-identity= and =mrr-relation=; neither depends on the other.

=mrr-identity= owns stable typed identities. =mrr-relation= owns relation
schemas, language-neutral values, and arity-checked facts. =mrr-query= owns
terms, atoms, and conjunctive query shape. =mrr-logic= owns safe rules.
=mrr-lineage= owns derivation support. =mrr-transition= owns semantic-generation deltas.
=mrr-bundle= is the validated transport and persistence admission boundary.
The =meta-relational-reasoning= crate is the public facade and owns no
duplicate semantics.

* Language adapters

The existing =gql-*= crates remain the GQL concrete-syntax and semantic
frontend. No =gql-reasoning= or =gql-ascent= compatibility authority exists.
GQL and Cypher are admitted only through executable =mrr-frontends=
syntax-to-MRR contracts, not aliases, provider shims, or empty placeholders.

* Runtime adapters

=mrr-ascent= and =mrr-gerbil= are downstream adapters. They may execute or
transport validated MRR bundles, but they cannot redefine identity, relation,
rule, transition, or lineage meaning. Gerbil compilation uses the reviewed
=gerbil-scheme-rust= integration boundary; Scheme is not evaluated on Rust
frontend hot paths.

* Repository topology

Stable architecture and audit material lives under =docs/architecture= and
=docs/audit=. Scheme grammar and reasoning owners live under =scheme=. Formal
proofs live under =proofs/MRRProof=. Domain fixtures live under
=fixtures/{software,knowledge,workflow,query,transition}=. Research notes and
benchmarks remain non-authoritative inputs under =research= and =benchmarks=.

Execution plans, milestone status, and supervisor receipts are task state and
must not be committed as repository documents.

* Admission sequence

This architecture admits the complete MRR contract chain, facade, the
executable =BundleAdmission.lean= proof, and its uv-managed local validation
adapter. The proof covers typed identity separation, bundle fact identity,
transition-insertion schema/arity, rule-relation admission, closure binding,
lineage/transition owner conjunction, and counterexample replay. Ascent,
Gerbil AOT, GQL/Cypher lowering, fixtures, differential oracles, and performance
owners are independently tested consumers of these contracts.

* Publication view

The Typst Babel block is the publication view of this Org authority. It is not
a second specification source; prose and contract changes are made in Org.

== Ownership

MRR identity, relation, query, logic, lineage, transition, and bundle semantics
belong to their corresponding `mrr-*` crates. GQL and Cypher are downstream
language adapters. Runtime integrations execute validated contracts and do not
redefine them.

== Admission

`mrr-bundle` validates the complete admitted graph, including base facts,
transition insertions, and rule relation references. The
`meta-relational-reasoning` crate is a facade without duplicate authority.

Let $R$ be the finite map from relation identities to arities, $F_0$ the base
facts, $T$ the transitions, and $P$ the rules. Let $rho(f)$ be a fact or atom's
relation identity and $nu(f)$ a fact's value count. For a fact $f$, define

$ "Valid"_R(f) := rho(f) in "dom"(R) and nu(f) = R(rho(f)). $

For a rule $p$ with head atom $h$ and body atoms $b_i$, define

$ "RuleValid"_R(p) := rho(h(p)) in "dom"(R) and
  forall i, rho(b_i(p)) in "dom"(R). $

The bundle admission predicate is

$ "BundleValid"(R, F_0, P, T) :=
  "Unique"("ids"(F_0 + "insertions"(T))) and
  (forall f in F_0, "Valid"_R(f)) and
  (forall t in T, forall f in "insertions"(t), "Valid"_R(f)) and
  (forall p in P, "RuleValid"_R(p)). $

Consequently, every fact made reachable by an admitted transition has a known
schema and the correct arity, every rule remains inside the admitted relation
universe, and no insertion can alias an already admitted fact identity. The
Lean development under `proofs/MRRProof` kernel-checks these consequences.
The Python local-proof adapter computes the transitive crate impact of current
or future changed paths, selects the corresponding theorem obligations, injects
the validated counterexample replay, hashes the exact generated source, and
kernel-checks it with pinned local Lean 4.31. No proof input crosses a network
boundary and no external proof service is an MRR authority.

== Topology

Lineage and transition are sibling contracts over identity and relation. The
canonical dependency graph is the Mermaid Babel block named
`rfc-0001-dependency-graph` in this Org document.
