* Sources consulted

Green, Karvounarakis, and Tannen establish semiring provenance as symbolic
bookkeeping for relational query results and discuss recursive Datalog through
fixed-point equations.  Later work emphasizes that recursive Datalog may have
infinitely many derivation trees and that competing provenance interpretations
must not be collapsed without an explicit semantic choice.

- [[https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf][Green et al., Provenance Semirings, PODS 2007]]
- [[https://proceedings.kr.org/2022/10/kr2022-0010-bourgaux-et-al.pdf][Bourgaux et al., Revisiting Semiring Provenance for Datalog, KR 2022]]

* MVP choice

MRR v1 does not claim a general provenance semiring.  It admits a finite,
acyclic, identity-bearing explanation graph after bounded execution and exact
lineage validation.  WHY is a projection over that admitted graph, not a second
derivation engine.

The default projection retains every admitted path reachable from the requested
result.  A deterministic single path is available only through an explicitly
named one-witness operation.  This avoids silently discarding alternate
derivations while leaving room for a future algebraic provenance layer.

* Deferred work

- recursive provenance represented by cyclic equations;
- multiplicities and semiring annotations;
- minimal witness enumeration and ranking;
- probabilistic or trust-valued provenance.

These are not compatibility paths.  They are absent from the MVP public
contract until their own authority and boundedness rules are admitted.
