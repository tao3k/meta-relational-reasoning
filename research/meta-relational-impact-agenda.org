#+title: Meta-Relational Impact: Research and Evaluation Agenda
#+startup: overview

* Research question and present baseline

Can MRR use typed relations *about admitted relations and derivations* to
compute a sound reevaluation frontier across semantic generations, retain
conclusions with independent support, and selectively reuse Context without
confusing graph reachability with temporal or causal impact?  [[file:../docs/architecture/0037-meta-relational-impact.org][RFC 0037]]
proposes the contract.  The question is deliberately broader than the
accepted [[file:../docs/architecture/0012-impact-projection.org][RFC 0012]] reverse lineage traversal.

The executable baseline is narrow.  =mrr-lineage::impact= walks predecessor
edges in an admitted acyclic graph; =mrr-agentic-ai-context= traverses the
union of old/new declared dependencies and denies reuse for reached nodes.
POO Flow separately implements structural Impact and exact descriptive or
candidate Trajectory contrast.  None of these baselines proves general
multi-support truth maintenance, closed-world absence, identified causal
effect, or a live token/KV cache saving.  The current PR's RFC 0035 and RFC
0036 propose Content authority and Context reuse; this agenda tests the
Impact connection without treating those proposals as shipped behavior.

* Competing models

| Model | Useful property | Counterexample or cost |
|-
| Reverse reachability only | simple finite frontier with path witness | a reached conclusion can survive through another derivation; a new fact may defeat an absence result without an old edge |
| Recompute everything | easy completeness story within a fixed closed inventory | expensive and hides which dependency or owner caused a change |
| Typed positive support sets, alternative branches, and explicit negative coverage | separates reevaluation from semantic retraction | requires complete support inventory, proposition/scope equivalence, and delta admission |
| Generic untyped graph of anything | easy to ingest arbitrary links | conflates evidence, policy, temporal possibility, and causal effect |

The proposed third model is admissible only if its extra checks earn their
cost.  Until then, conservative recomputation is the safe runtime behavior.
Typed meta relations are a way to preserve the distinction between evidence
levels, not a claim that every source graph should be loaded into MRR.

* Hypotheses and falsifiers

1. *Frontier coverage.* For every changed declared positive dependency in a
   finite old/new graph, the dependent evaluator is reached.  Falsify with a
   removed edge, new edge, changed rule/schema, or changed authority binding
   omitted from the frontier.
2. *Support preservation.* A proposition with two complete applicable
   derivations remains eligible when only one loses an input, provided their
   output-key equivalence and scopes are checked.  Falsify with an output
   marked retracted while the second branch is current and admitted, or with
   two merely equal =FactId=s accepted as equivalent without that check.
3. *Retraction soundness.* Marking a proposition retracted requires a complete
   relevant support inventory.  Falsify with an undiscovered branch or a
   scope mismatch between branches.
4. *Bounded absence.* An added matching fact can defeat a certified negative
   premise, and a removed one can enable a result.  Falsify with a zero-row
   query treated as complete without a coverage certificate, or an addition
   invisible to the old reverse graph.
5. *Authority separation.* A POO structural witness or candidate contrast
   remains at its declared claim level after being related in MRR.  Falsify
   with an MRR result or action guard treating that receipt as an identified
   intervention effect or runtime authorization.
6. *Reuse eligibility.* A revoked capability or revised contract denies
   current semantic use even when Content bytes and rendered prompt bytes
   agree.  Falsify with a cross-actor or post-revocation disclosure.

These remain implementation hypotheses, not measured runtime results.  The
[[file:../proofs/MRRProof/AgenticAIContext/MRR/AgenticAIContext/MetaImpact.lean][Lean model]] proves conditional support and absence laws; the
[[file:../proofs/MRRProof/AgenticAIContextTLA/MetaImpact.tla][TLA+ model]] checks finite cases with explicit counterexample mutants.  An
integration fixture must still show that actual producers and effect owners
enforce the cut and authority checks.

* Fixture matrix

| Fixture | Old -> new change | Expected result |
|-
| Two derivations of =Q= | one support removed | =Q= reached, then revalidated through the other complete branch |
| Last derivation | its final input removed | retracted only if support inventory is complete |
| New policy prohibition | item inserted into a certified applicable domain | old absence result reevaluated; compliance owner decides the new verdict |
| Incomplete query inventory | zero rows with partial coverage | absence remains unknown |
| New relation edge | endpoint dependency added at =g1= | new dependent enters frontier even though no old path exists |
| Authority revision | capability revoked, same source bytes | old Context/action receipt stale; byte equality gives no authorization |
| POO structural Impact | relation inventory incomplete | MRR retains partial scope and cannot certify unaffected nodes |
| POO trajectory revision | exact aligned series changes | POO refresh is recomputed or blocked according to its own scope/grid rules; MRR records receipt change only |

The fixtures should preserve original Fact/Relation IDs, source revisions,
generation cuts, old/new graph inventories, and producer receipts.  Each case
must state whether the oracle checks frontier inclusion, semantic eligibility,
Temporal classification, or an actual action guard.  A failure in one layer
cannot be called a pass because another layer's receipt exists.

* Evaluation plan

Implement a fixed baseline of RFC 0012 traversal plus full recomputation for
all reached outputs, then compare against typed selective reevaluation on the
same graphs, rules, source changes, policy cuts, and hardware.  Report:

- false negatives and false positives in the frontier against exhaustive
  recomputation over a *declared complete* fixture inventory;
- incorrect retraction, unsupported revalidation, missed addition, and stale
  reuse as separate semantic errors;
- number of reevaluated owners/derivations and receipt bytes;
- CPU, wall time, peak memory, and end-to-end action latency separately;
- source and Temporal producer cost separately from MRR traversal and
  evaluator cost; and
- for Context, selected nodes, serialized bytes/tokens, token-prefix
  eligibility, actual serving-cache hits, and authorization denials separately.

Control or record host contention and cache warm-up.  Use identical inputs,
toolchain, budget, and verifier versions for A/B runs.  A performance claim
requires measured improvement with no semantic regression.  Frontier
soundness is conditional on declared dependency completeness; general-world
unaffectedness and causal identification are outside this evaluation.

* Sequence and open gates

1. Specify tagged meta-reference and edge-role admission with negative tests.
2. Implement positive, acyclic, two-generation support reevaluation and
   migrate RFC 0012 callers directly to the new result and field names.
3. Model alternative support and retraction conditions in the existing MRR
   proof project; check old/new edge and authority changes.
4. Add finite-domain negative-premise certificates and insertion/deletion
   deltas; reject unbounded or uncertified absence.
5. Import POO Flow owner receipts at their exact claim levels; test scope,
   cut, producer, and digest mismatch.
6. Connect typed Content and Context receipts to the actual action/reuse
   owners, then run controlled A/B evaluation.

The Temporal effect and counterfactual levels remain POO Flow research gates.
The MRR proposal can be useful without implementing either one: it can say
which evidence and projections must be reevaluated when a Temporal receipt
changes, while leaving the Temporal verdict to its owner.
